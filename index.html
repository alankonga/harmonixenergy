Questions

Good to know before you start

How do I know what size solar system I need?

Start with your electricity bill or a list of the appliances you use. Our load calculator and savings estimator give you a realistic starting size in minutes, and we confirm it at a free site assessment.

Can solar work together with my KPLC supply?

Yes. A hybrid system uses solar first, draws from the grid when needed and keeps essential loads running during outages. Grid-tied and fully off-grid designs are also available.

What happens during a power cut?

A system with batteries keeps the circuits you choose running automatically. A grid-tied system without batteries switches off for safety during an outage, so tell us if backup matters to you.

Do you maintain systems after installation?

Yes. We offer inspections, preventive maintenance and fault finding for systems we install and for many installed by others.

How long does installation take?

Most homes can be completed in a few days once equipment is on site. Larger commercial and industrial projects are scheduled after the design is approved. We confirm the timeline in your quotation.
